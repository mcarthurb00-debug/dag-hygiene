Company Overview

Dedicated to Hygiene Excellence

DAG Hygiene Ltd. is a professional cleaning and hygiene solutions company specializing in commercial, residential, and industrial cleaning. We provide high-quality sanitation services, post-construction cleaning, general hygiene supply, and large-scale institutional cleaning.

Our mission is to create clean, safe, and healthy environments for homes, businesses, and public institutions. We combine modern cleaning equipment, trained staff, and industry-standard hygiene practices to deliver exceptional results.
